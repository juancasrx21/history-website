The Evolution of the Two Main Political Parties in Belize

Two Parties, One Belize

Brief Overview

This website dives into the story of Belize's two major political parties, the People's United Party (PUP) and the United Democratic Party (UDP), through the eyes of the people. We explore how these parties began, how their beliefs and goals have changed over time, and how everyday Belizeans have responded to those shifts. From the early push for independence to the political challenges we face today, we look at how these parties have shaped national life and how public opinion has shaped them in return.

Rationale Behind the Project

We chose this topic because we wanted to understand more than just politics. We wanted to hear what people really think. Political decisions impact everything from the economy to education, and public trust plays a big role in how our country moves forward. Through interviews and research, we aim to highlight the voices of Belizeans and show how leadership, power, and public opinion continue to influence the direction of our nation.
